The information boundary

Each day’s forecast is issued at 10:00 Copenhagen time on the previous day. Price features become usable 24 hours after their delivery interval ends. Calendar features are known in advance. No future weather or realized target-day generation enters the model.

Training and calibration are separate. Monthly refits can use earlier test-period prices once available. Source revisions remain a limitation: this is an archived-data replay, not a verified historical publication-vintage study.

The limits matter

One summer-to-autumn test window does not establish year-round performance. The uncertainty bands miss their 80% target in this run. Weather, outages, interconnector constraints and forward market information are absent.

Flexible consumption is hypothetical; continuity, ramp limits, tariffs, imbalance charges and market access are not modeled. The hindsight cost is a lower bound, not an achievable strategy.
